# Governance

## Roles

- **Contributor:** submits issues, designs, documentation, tests, or code.
- **Maintainer:** reviews and merges changes, triages reports, and stewards one area.
- **Release maintainer:** participates in the threshold release process after a track
  record of maintenance and security training.

No person becomes a maintainer solely by funding the project. Maintainers are added
by consensus of existing maintainers based on sustained, constructive work. At least
two maintainers approve changes to signing, key storage, derivation, dependencies,
CI permissions, or release infrastructure.

## Decision process

Small reversible changes use normal pull-request review. Security boundaries, new
chains, governance, licensing, and irreversible choices require a public design
proposal with at least seven days for review. Maintainers seek consensus; when that
fails, a two-thirds vote of non-conflicted maintainers decides.

## Independence

The project does not sell placement, collect user data, issue a token, accept paid
asset listings, or add financial services as a condition of sponsorship. Material
funding and conflicts of interest are disclosed publicly.

## Removal

Maintainer access may be suspended immediately for credible account compromise.
Removal for conduct or prolonged inactivity requires a documented maintainer decision
and a reasonable opportunity to respond, except where doing so increases security risk.
