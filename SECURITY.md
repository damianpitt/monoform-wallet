# Security policy

## Supported versions

There is no production release. All current code is pre-alpha and must not be used
with real funds.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Use the repository's **Security** tab and select
**Report a vulnerability** to open a private security advisory. Include:

- affected commit or version;
- impact and realistic attack path;
- reproduction steps or proof of concept;
- any suggested mitigation;
- whether you plan to disclose and on what timeline.

The project aims to acknowledge complete reports within three working days and agree
on a disclosure timeline after triage. These are targets, not a guarantee at this
pre-alpha stage.

Never include an actual seed phrase, private key, or real-funds transaction in a report.

## Safe harbor intent

Good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction, service
degradation, and real-funds interaction is welcome. We will not pursue legal action
against research consistent with this policy. This statement does not bind third parties.
