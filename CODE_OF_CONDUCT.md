# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a focused, respectful, harassment-free project. Debate ideas and
evidence vigorously; treat people with patience and dignity. Harassment, threats,
doxxing, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, and sustained personal attacks
are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation
when conduct undermines safety or productive collaboration. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters.

Until a dedicated conduct address exists, report concerns privately to the repository
owner through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be replaced by a
formally adopted version as the maintainer group forms.
